# Ingredient thumbnails

Curated from Ingredient Atlas by ionmesca: https://github.com/ionmesca/ingredient-atlas.
Atlas metadata and AI-generated images are CC0-1.0. Source URLs and original SHA-256 checksums are retained in data/ingredients.json. Thumbnails were resized to 256px WebP.
The generic bowl is a Sirvya vector illustration. Prepared ingredients without suitable artwork use that neutral image. Nutrition data is not imported.
